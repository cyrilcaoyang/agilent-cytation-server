# Bench session — 2026-09-08

Supervised session on `sdl2-pc-03-cytation`, operator at the instrument. Purpose
was the item [`BENCH_2026-09-04.md`](BENCH_2026-09-04.md) §5 left open: **is
there an objective in the light path?** There is not.

The service was stopped for the inspection and the instrument was not commanded
while it was open. Findings below are physical observation plus the
absorbance cross-check that preceded it.

## 1. The plate, confirmed spectroscopically

Operator-stated layout: methylene blue concentrated in A2 and E2 with serial
dilutions down column 4 (D4-C4-B4-A4 and H4-G4-F4-E4), Rhodamine 6G similarly
in column 9, water in column 6, and a suspension of an emissive solid in water
in column 12.

Read back before the plate was disturbed, and it agrees:

| well | expected | 664 nm | 526 nm |
|---|---|---|---|
| A2 | methylene blue, concentrated | **over range** | 0.8117 |
| H4 | blue series, top | **over range** | 0.7402 |
| A4 | blue series, dilute | 1.1435 | 0.3149 |
| A9 / H9 | Rhodamine 6G | 0.209 / 0.219 | **2.27 / 2.90** |
| A12 / H12 | emissive suspension | 0.375 / 0.414 | 0.501 / 0.543 |
| A1, A6 | blank / water | 0.204 / 0.196 | 0.283 / 0.261 |

Three things worth keeping:

* **Orientation is unchanged.** A2 and H4 still saturate and column 9 still
  spikes at 526; a plate reinserted 180° out would have moved those to H11 and
  column 4. A4 read **1.1435** against **1.1434** on 2026-09-06 — four decimal
  places, three days apart.
* **Column 12 is scattering, not absorbing** — elevated equally at both
  wavelengths with no band. That was inferred from the 09-07 perimeter sweep
  before the operator said what was in it, and the suspension explains it.
* **H6 disagrees with "water".** A6 reads blank; H6 is 0.726 / 0.768, the same
  flat-offset signature as the suspension column. Something is scattering in
  that well — bubble, condensation, or carry-over. Not chased.

## 2. The objective turret — no objectives fitted

Turret positions are numbered 1-6 (`▼1`, `6▲`, `2▲ ▼3` visible), matching
`objective_slots: 6`.

* **One position holds a large unmarked convex lens.** Front element far larger
  than any microscope objective's, no colour band, no series/magnification/NA
  engraving. Olympus marks every objective they make; this is not one.
* **One position held a `4X`-marked blanking plug** with a small central
  aperture. Removed, photographed, refitted.
* **The remaining positions are empty.**
* **The filter cube turret is empty** across its bores, matching
  `installed_filters: []`.

### This resolves everything left open on 09-04

| observation | explained by |
|---|---|
| 4X field spans 13.9 mm, exactly 4.00x PyLabRobot's 3.474 mm figure | there is no 4X |
| 19 frames across 9 mm of *accepted* Z travel, identical | nothing to bring into focus |
| effective ~0.6x (5.68 µm/px against 9 mm well pitch) | a wide-field relay lens, not an objective |
| 20X and 40X give one identical blank gradient | those positions are empty bores |
| autofocus "climbing noise" | no sharpness gradient exists |

The focus-encoding bug fixed on 09-04 was real and worth fixing — the axis
moves, and the instrument acknowledges each move with travel time proportional
to distance. It simply had nothing to focus.

### `installed_objectives` has never described the turret

PyLabRobot identifies objectives on firmware 2.x by reading **condenser phase
annulus** part numbers via `i h2`..`i h7`, then mapping them
(`1320520` → 4X, `1320521` → 20X, `1322026` → 40X). The operator physically
removed one of those annuli during this session. The instrument therefore has
annuli for 4X, 20X and 40X and no objectives to pair them with, while
`/status` has been reporting three fitted objectives throughout.

Note this differs by firmware family: on v1 (Cytation 1) the backend reads
`i o1`/`i o2` and decodes genuine *objective* part-number strings, which does
identify objectives. A [PyLabRobot forum thread][plr] about missing Cytation 1
objectives concerns that v1 path and does not apply here.

[plr]: https://discuss.pylabrobot.org/t/cannot-find-the-cytation-1-objectives-with-the-cytation-5-backend/182/17

## 4. Stale plate assertion, observed live

The operator removed the plate and a second user took the instrument. The
service continued to report `plate_in_reader: true` for
`square96_19mm_20260904` and kept offering all three reads plus
`imaging.capture` — reads on an empty carrier would have returned numbers.

This is the "stale `in`" direction of the drawer/plate dead-reckoning problem
already recorded in [`../HANDOFF.md`](../HANDOFF.md), seen in the wild rather
than reasoned about. Cleared with `plate.unload` (bookkeeping only, no motion),
after which optical actions were correctly withheld.

## 5. Front-panel eject is not detectable — tested 2026-09-09

Recorded in `HANDOFF.md` as impossible on the strength of the command set
(`J` opens, `A` closes, nothing reports position). Now measured rather than
assumed.

Baseline established with `ftdi_trace = true` and an empty carrier: idle
traffic is a strict two-frame cycle, `TX h` then `RX <ACK>2450000<ETX>`, every
4.29 s and nothing else. The operator then pressed the front-panel eject out
and back in.

**330 trace events across the press, zero unsolicited bytes.** Every frame was
the temperature poll or its reply, and the poll cadence never faltered
(4.31 / 4.34 / 4.33 s across the event). The firmware does not treat the press
as an event on this link.

Afterwards the service still reported `drawer: in` and `plate_stage: in` with
the carrier physically moved — the stale-`in` case, observed directly. Harmless
on this occasion only because no plate was registered, so optical actions were
already withheld.

**Consequence:** detection is not available, so the mitigation must be the
ensure-closed approach in `HANDOFF.md` — send `A` before an optical action when
the drawer belief is merely assumed rather than commanded. Nothing else can
close this gap.

## Consequences

Imaging capability is a **purchasing question**, not a software one — see
[`FLUORESCENCE_IMAGING.md`](FLUORESCENCE_IMAGING.md). Ordered 2026-09-09:
4X phase objective `1320515`, whose annulus is already fitted. Fluorescence
imaging additionally needs a filter cube and its matching LED cube, and the
band should be chosen from a measured excitation/emission scan of the actual
emitter rather than guessed.

Until an objective is fitted, **every image in `captures/` is an uncalibrated
~0.6x overview**. They are usable as whole-well survey — the 08-31 comparison
of D8 (needles, transmitting) against D3 (opaque) still holds — but nothing
should be sized from them.
