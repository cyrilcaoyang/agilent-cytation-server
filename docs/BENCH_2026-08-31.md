absorbance and fluorescence both read H12 (0.0804 and 254 respectively).

### Why it is not the checksum band

PLR builds the luminescence command in
`agilent/biotek_backend.py::read_luminescence` and checksums it as
`(sum(cmd.encode()) + 8) % 100`. Computed against the observations:

| Region | Checksum | In 94-99? | Observed |
|---|---|---|---|
| H11 | 99 | yes | passed |
| G12 | 99 | yes | passed |
| B7 | 97 | yes | passed |
| D3 | 93 | no | passed |
| A1 | 83 | no | passed |
| H12 | 01 | no | **failed** |
| H12+H11 | 00 | no | **failed** |
| G11..H12 | 99 | yes | **failed** |
| A1..H12 | 92 | no | **failed** |

The band predicts the opposite of what happens, so `_pad_for_checksum` is the
wrong tool and extending it to luminescence would not help. The two distinct
timings (0.1 s for a single well, ~12 s for a region) mean the command is
rejected at the start command in one case and the response assertion fails in
the other — possibly two causes.

## 5. Imaging

Nine brightfield captures, all 200. Autofocus and auto-exposure ran on a real
subject for the first time; auto-exposure converged to peak pixel 197-202 out
of 255, which is its ~80 % target, and settled on 4.43 ms in every well
(expected — the peak pixel is set by background illumination, not the sample).

Visual confirmation that the path produces usable data: **D8 (citric acid,
A600 0.098) shows needle crystals with the well transmitting light, while D3
(KNO3, A600 1.61) is fully opaque.** Absorbance and imaging agree.

### The 40X objective returns the 20X image

Same well, three objectives, autofocus and auto-exposure on:

| Requested | Reported | Time | mean | std |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `O_4X_PL_FL_Phase` | 4X | 4.1 s | 143.16 | 54.72 |
| `O_20X_PL_FL_Phase` | 20X | 11.8 s | 170.36 | 16.40 |
| `O_40X_PL_FL_Phase` | 40X | 5.0 s | 170.40 | 16.40 |

Pairwise differences: 4X vs 20X mean |diff| 36.32 (12 % of pixels within 2
grey levels); **20X vs 40X mean |diff| 2.11, identical std, 65 % within 2** —
the same frame plus sensor noise. The timing agrees: the turret took 11.8 s to
move for 20X and 5.0 s for 40X, matching a capture with no turret change.

Autofocus also returned the identical focal height (8.082841185525986) for 4X
and 20X, and repeated the same value across unrelated wells, so it is
quantized to a fixed search grid rather than searching per objective.

### Open: the 4X field is far wider than 4X

A 4X capture spans about three wells (~20 mm). At 9 mm well pitch the measured
scale is ~8.15 µm/px, i.e. an effective ~0.42X. On a 2448×2048 sensor with
3.45 µm pixels a true 4X would give ~2.1 mm, a patch inside one well.

Not asserted as a bug — the sensor geometry is assumed, not read from the
camera. **Settle it with a calibration target** (known feature spacing, or the
vendor objective setup plate PN 1222531), not a crystallization plate. Until
then, treat image scale as unknown: anyone sizing crystals from these captures
could be out by roughly 10x.
