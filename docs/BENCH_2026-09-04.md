# Bench session — 2026-09-04

Driving the live service on port 8040 through `/control/*` with a held claim,
then the backend directly (service stopped) for the turret probe. Purpose was
to settle what "the limitations of the Cytation 5" actually are, after the
2026-09-03 sweep concluded that `focal_height_mm` does not move the focus.
Summary in [`../HANDOFF.md`](../HANDOFF.md); this file is the evidence.

The instrument of the day was the **D2XX byte trace** (`[instrument].ftdi_trace
= true`, reassembled with `scripts/trace_frames.py`). Every conclusion below
rests on what the instrument replied, not on what the driver assumed. Two
things about the trace worth knowing before reading one: it logs one line per
*read byte* but one line per *write call*, so a byte-only parse silently drops
every command parameter (the first two passes at this missed the answer for
exactly that reason); and the idle baseline is a single `h` temperature poll
answered `<ACK>2450000<ETX>` every ~4.4 s, so anything unsolicited stands out.

## 1. The focus command was malformed below 9.3993 mm — FIXED

PyLabRobot's `set_focus` builds `F{mode}0{counts:05d}`: a literal `0` and five
digits. The instrument's position field is **seven** digits, so that shape is
only right when `counts` needs six of them. Traced, unpatched, 4X, well H12:

| `focal_height_mm` | sent | field | reply | latency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.50 | `F5047876` | 6 digits | `570F` | 15 ms |
| 5.50 | `F5058515` | 6 | `570F` | 31 ms |
| 6.50 | `F5069154` | 6 | `570F` | 36 ms |
| 7.50 | `F5079793` | 6 | `570F` | 15 ms |
| 8.50 | `F5090432` | 6 | `570F` | 16 ms |
| 9.00 | `F5095751` | 6 | `570F` | 15 ms |
| 9.40 | `F50100007` | 7 | `0000` | 93 ms |
| 10.50 | `F50111710` | 7 | `0000` | 187 ms |
| 12.00 | `F50127668` | 7 | `0000` | 204 ms |
| 13.50 | `F50143627` | 7 | `0000` | 200 ms |

The rejected form answers a constant status in a time too short to contain any
travel. The accepted form answers `0000` after a delay that scales with the
distance moved. `counts` crosses 100000 at f = 9.3993 mm, so the driver's own
declared range (4.5–13.88) was 68 % unusable, and PLR discards the reply, so
the rejection was invisible.

**The 2026-09-03 sweep contains this in hindsight.** Its 4X series is flat
to ±0.03 in each half and has exactly one step — std 43.61 → 48.95 — between
f=9.00 and f=9.50; its 0.01 mm boundary sweep steps between 9.39 and 9.40, at
17× its own noise. Both were read as noise because the comparison baseline was
wrong: the stage sat where the last *accepted* command left it, which was the
previous sweep's 13.50.

`CytationReader._set_focus` now emits `F{mode}{counts:07d}` and checks the
reply. Re-traced after the restart, same ten heights: all `0000`, 13.50 → 4.50
took 622 ms, each 1.0 mm step 187–188 ms, 0.4 mm 123 ms. The encoding is
byte-identical to PLR's above the boundary (`tests/test_focus_command.py` pins
that), so the range that had a hardware track record cannot regress.

## 2. `570F` is "position refused", not "malformed field" — and the window is per-objective

With the encoding fixed, `F50074473` (7.0 mm, correctly formed) is still
refused at 20X. Mapped through the API, well D6, exposure irrelevant (no
light needed for the status):

```
O_4X_PL_FL_Phase    ##########   every 1.0 mm step 4.5 -> 13.5 accepted
O_20X_PL_FL_Phase   ..........   none
O_40X_PL_FL_Phase   ..........   none
```

20X re-probed at **0.1 mm steps, 94 positions, 19 s: none accepted.** Unchanged
across declared plate heights of 7.5 / 14.5 / 19.0 mm (`agilent_shallow_96`,
`custom_96`, `square_96_19mm`), and unchanged with the carrier empty and lit.
PyLabRobot's single global `focal_height_range` describes the 4X position
only. **No 20X or 40X frame in this repo has ever been in focus, and none
could have been** — see §5 for what those positions actually are.

## 3. Zero light was the plate, not the instrument

The first plate of the day (`agilent_96_700ul_square_flat`, opaque white PP)
gave mean 14.87–14.91 in all 54 captures: 2 → 500 ms, LED 1 vs 10 (14.899 vs
14.898), all objectives, phase contrast vs brightfield, several wells. Four hot
pixels in five million. Empty carrier, 4X: 2 ms → mean 96.2, 10 ms → 254.9,
50 ms → 255. Roughly **OD 5.7**. No software change alters that; brightfield
transmission imaging needs a clear-bottom plate.

## 4. The 4X image does not depend on Z at all

19 mm square-well plate (`square96_19mm_20260904`, particulate in H12), 4X,
auto-exposure settled at 8.1 ms, then fixed. 19 frames 4.5 → 13.5 mm, every
command accepted and every one a real move (§1):

```
f=4.50  lap_var 25.11  std 48.76  mean 59.47
  ...   25.07-25.20    48.76-49.17 (monotonic creep)   59.43-59.61
f=13.50 lap_var 25.13  std 49.17  mean 59.43
4.50 vs 13.50: mean|diff| 1.46, 81 % of pixels within 2
```

A 4X objective has ~50 µm depth of field; 9 mm of travel through one goes
from sharp to featureless. This frame has effectively infinite depth of field,
and it spans ~13.9 mm — adjacent well centres 1580–1587 px apart at 9 mm pitch
gives 5.68 µm/px, and PLR's own `capture()` puts a 4X frame at 3.474 mm.
13.9 / 3.474 = **4.00**. Whatever forms this image, it is not a 4X objective.

## 5. Turret probe — five positions, one frame

Service stopped, backend driven directly (`scripts/probe_objective_slots.py`).
For each `P0e{code}`: select, re-select H12 with that code in the `Z` command,
probe ten raw focus counts spanning 10 000 – 800 000, grab one 8 ms frame.

| code | move | focus accepted | frame (mean / std / lap_var) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 61 ms (already there) | 10 000 → 300 000; 500 000+ `570F` | plate visible: 59.3 / 49.9 / 25.1 |
| 2 | 9.0 s | none | gradient: 145.9 / 21.7 / 73.9 |
| 3 | 1.4 s | none | identical |
| 4 | 1.3 s | none | identical |
| 5 | 1.4 s | none | identical |
| 6 | 1.3 s | none | identical |

Pairwise mean|diff| among codes 2–6: **1.95** — one frame plus sensor noise,
five times, including the two positions the inventory calls empty. The frame
is a smooth left-to-right illumination gradient (177 → 116 across the width)
with no plate structure.

Inventory, straight from the instrument: `h2 = 1320520`, `h3 = 1320521`,
`h4 = 1322026`, `h5–h7 = **********`. Those are **phase-annulus part numbers
from the condenser** — the illumination side. PLR maps them to
`O_4X/20X/40X_PL_FL_Phase` and calls the result "installed objectives". It has
never been evidence of glass in the objective turret.

What the two facts together say: codes 2–6 do not behave like three different
objectives plus two empty slots. Every optical observation since August fits
**no objective in the light path at any turret position** — code 1 an open
aperture (the tube lens alone gives the ~0.6× field of §4, Z-independent),
codes 2–6 turret body with a light leak. Whether the objectives are missing,
fitted where the codes don't reach, or present with a misrouted camera path is
a physical inspection, not a software question. **Open at end of session.**

Frames and `summary.json`: `captures/20260904T1850_slot_probe/` (gitignored).

## 6. What this retires from `BENCH_2026-08-31.md`

- **"The 40X objective returns the 20X image."** The turret moves for every
  code (`P0e01/02/03` all `0000`, 1.3 / 8.3 / 1.3 s of travel). The identical
  frames were the §5 gradient — the same for all five non-1 positions.
- **"Autofocus is quantized to a fixed search grid."** It was climbing noise
  through rejected commands (§1); every recorded value ≥ 9.4 was reached by a
  command the instrument never accepted.
- **"Settle the 4X field with a calibration target."** The factor is exactly
  4.00 against PLR's own figure, no sensor assumption needed (§4).
- **The image-diff signature** (mean|diff| ≈ 2 = "same frame") is not a test
  of anything on its own — it separates frames that differ only in focus just
  as poorly (§4).

Still standing from that day: the luminescence H12 refusal, the checksum
94–99 band, incubator unreadable while shaking, no eject lockout, and four
empty filter-cube slots.

## 7. Carrier and drawer, while the trace was on

- **`5A00` is not an error.** `J` (open) → `0000` in ~0.5–2 s; `A` (close) →
  `5A00` after ~6 s of motion; `W6rrcc` (select well) → `5A00` after 5.4 s.
  It is the carrier stage's "motion complete", regardless of whether a plate
  is registered (`y set_plate` before `A` changes nothing).
- **Manual insert with the front-panel button** left the service reading
  `drawer: out` while the carrier was in. `POST /control/drawer/close` is the
  resync — idempotent at the firmware level, already in `allowed_actions`
  whenever we believe the carrier is out. HANDOFF carries the design options.
- **Not tested:** whether the instrument volunteers any bytes when the eject
  button is pressed. The baseline (one `h` pair per 4.4 s) makes it a
  one-press test; it did not happen this session.
