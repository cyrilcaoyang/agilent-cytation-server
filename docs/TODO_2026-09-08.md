## Lab priorities

1. **Resolve the optical path first.** Stop the service before using Gen5;
   retain the FTDI vendor driver. Confirm the fitted/configured optics and
   acquire a focused image with credible scale on a suitable clear-bottom
   target. If Gen5 cannot do this, investigate fit-out/configuration before
   additional driver focus sweeps. Close Gen5 before restarting the service.
2. **Compare identical Gen5/API settings.** Match the actual plate model,
   objective, well, exposure, gain, and a small set of nearby focus positions.
   September 8 evidence shows that declared plate height affects the reachable
   read-focus floor: on the tested 19 mm model, 4.5–5.6 mm failed with `5B00`
   and 5.8 mm and above worked. This is evidence for that setup, not a universal
   range. Never falsify the plate model to bypass a refusal.
3. **Resolve the luminescence boundary.** Compare H11, G12, H12, and full plate
   in Gen5 and the API, one case at a time, using a demonstrated reachable
   focal height. Save request, reply, timings, and trace. The new `5B00`
   diagnosis does not prove the older H12 failure is resolved.
4. **Check quantitative-read consumers.** Verify that workflows and plotting
   code inspect `over_range` and treat those wells as saturated. A `null`
   must not silently disappear from a calibration fit. Use an appropriate
   blank/reference and an unsaturated measurement for comparison with Gen5.
5. **Then qualify incubation/shaking.** On a dedicated liquid test plate,
   measure time to reach and hold the chosen setpoint; perform a short observed
   shake and verify recovery after stopping. Pause shaking before temperature
   reads. Defer unattended campaigns until these checks pass.

The API helper and examples are in [IMPLEMENTATION.md](IMPLEMENTATION.md).
